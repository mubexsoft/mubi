What are MUBİ English Apps?

MUBİ English Apps are English learning and practice apps for students in grades 2–8. Flashcards, quizzes and listening activities help reinforce what students learn at school.

Why don’t we use pictures?

MUBİ In vocabulary activities, we focus on spelling, Turkish meaning and pronunciation. This lets you practise a word’s meaning and sound together instead of linking it only to a picture. MUBİ Our characters bring colour to the interface while the flashcards offer a simple study space.

Are MUBİ English Apps free?

Yes! MUBİ You can download and use the English Apps for free. Get started by choosing your grade in the download section.

Do I need an account to use MUBİ English Apps?

No. MUBİ You do not need to create an account. Download the app for your grade and start using the content.

What makes MUBİ English Apps different?

MUBİ The most important difference is that the English Apps follow the content of the school textbook exactly. There is a separate app for each grade from 2 to 8. Revise the units and words you learn at school with flashcards, two-way quizzes, listening and gap-fill activities. Study at your own pace without an account or paid subscription.

Do MUBİ English Apps collect personal information?

MUBİ You do not need an account, so we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number for registration. Advertising services may process technical data such as device information and advertising identifiers. See the privacy and data safety details on the relevant store page.

Is there a paid subscription in MUBİ English Apps?

No. MUBİ You do not need a paid subscription or a monthly or annual payment to use the English Apps.

Will MUBİ English Apps become paid in the future?

MUBİ We plan to continue offering the English Apps free with advertising support. Our aim is to make English learning content available without a paid subscription.

Why are MUBİ English Apps free?

We want more students to access English learning and revision activities. Advertising in the apps MUBİ helps keep the English Apps free.

How much advertising is in MUBİ English Apps?

MUBİ The English Apps contain ads that help keep them free. Share your experience and suggestions about advertising with @mubi.ingilizce.

Which grades are MUBİ English Apps for?

MUBİ There are separate apps for grades 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8. Choose your grade and practise with content for that level.

How do I download MUBİ English Apps?
  1. In “Choose your grade. Start!”, select the app for your grade.
  2. Open the Google Play or App Store page for your device.
  3. Download and open the app, then choose the theme or unit you want to study.

You can also open Google Play or the App Store and search for “MUBİ 2. Sınıf”, “MUBİ 3. Sınıf” or the name of your grade to find and download the app.

Can I use the apps on Android and iPhone?

Yes. MUBİ Links to Google Play and the App Store are available on this page. Check the relevant store page for device compatibility and operating system requirements.

Is the content aligned with school lessons and the curriculum?

MUBİ The English Apps offer themes and units aligned with the English curriculum for each grade. Choose the topic you are studying at school and revise it in the app.

What activities are included?

English and Turkish flashcards, English-to-Turkish and Turkish-to-English quizzes, listening quizzes and gap-fill activities. Select an activity from the unit screen.

How do I use the flashcards?

Choose a theme or unit and open the flashcards. Look at the word, tap the audio button to hear its pronunciation and reveal its meaning. Mark whether you know the word.

How do I find listening activities?

Open the unit you want to study and select the English Listening Quiz card. Follow the on-screen instructions to start practising.

What are the quizzes for?

Quizzes help you recall words and match them with their meanings. Take a quiz after studying the flashcards to reinforce what you have learned.

Do MUBİ English Apps offer live lessons or teachers?

MUBİ Study at your own pace with prepared flashcards, quizzes and listening content. The apps do not provide live lessons or one-to-one teaching.

Where should I start?

Download the app for your grade and choose the theme you are studying at school. Start with flashcards, then revise with quizzes and listening activities. Set a study time that works for you and practise regularly.

What should I do when I move to the next grade?

Choose the app for your new grade in the MUBİ download section on this page. Each grade has its own store links.

How can I report a problem or share a suggestion?

Send a message on Instagram to @mubi.ingilizce and tell us which grade app and device you use, and which screen you are on. You can follow the same account for new posts.
