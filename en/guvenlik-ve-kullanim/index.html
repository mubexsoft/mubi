Download from the official store

Use the Google Play and App Store links on this site to find the MUBİ English app for your grade. Store accounts and downloads are subject to the relevant store’s terms.

What the apps offer

MUBİ English Apps provide prepared flashcards, quizzes and listening activities. They do not offer live lessons or teacher-led tuition. They are study tools to help revise school lessons.

Free usage and advertising

Downloading and using the apps is free. No account or paid subscription is required. The apps contain ads.

For families

Choose the app for your child’s grade and manage usage and store settings with your device’s parental controls.
